2. How We Use Information

  • Account management: Create, maintain, and authenticate user accounts via social login
  • Service delivery: AI-powered family-tailored meal plans, shopping list management, favorite recipes
  • Community features: Posts, comments, likes, follows, reporting/blocking
  • Push notifications: Notify users of new followers, comments, etc. (toggleable in settings)
  • Advertising: Show ads via AdMob (revenue and AI-call abuse mitigation)
  • Service improvement: Crash analysis and usage analytics to improve features

3. Data Retention

We retain personal information while the user maintains an active account. When a user deletes their account via the in-app "Delete Account" feature, we permanently delete the following:

  • User account information (Firebase Auth)
  • User profile (nickname, photos, etc.)
  • User-generated posts, comments, favorites, meal/shopping data
  • Household membership (if last member, the entire household and its data are deleted)
  • Follow relationships and push notification tokens

Where applicable law requires retention (e.g., e-commerce or telecom regulations), we may retain data for the legally mandated period in a separate secure store before deletion.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell personal information. We share data only in the following cases:

  • With your prior consent
  • When required by law or lawful legal process
  • Within the community feature, posts, comments, and profiles you create are publicly visible to other users

6. AI Feature Notice

To generate family-tailored meal plans and recipes, we send the following information to the Gemini API operated by Google LLC:

  • Each family member's name, gender, age, nationality, notes, allergies, and disliked foods
  • Any request you type when generating a meal plan

This transfer happens only after you explicitly consent in the app. The first time you use an AI feature we show you exactly what will be sent and ask for your consent; without consent, AI features are unavailable. You can withdraw consent at any time under "Settings → AI data sharing", after which we will ask again the next time you use an AI feature.

The data is used solely to generate meal plans and recipes and is never used for advertising. Google processes this data per their own policies.

7. Advertising Identifier and Ads

The Service shows AdMob (Google) ads.

  • Android: Your advertising identifier (AAID) may be used to serve and measure ads. Go to Settings → Google → Ads to reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.
  • iOS: The Service does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA). We therefore do not track you across other companies' apps and websites, and only non-personalized ads are shown.

8. Your Rights

  • Access & Update: Edit your data in "My Page", "Edit Profile", and "Family Settings" within the app.
  • Deletion: Use "Settings → Delete Account" to permanently delete your account and related data. No support contact required.
  • Notification Opt-out: "Settings → Notification Settings" lets you toggle notification types individually.
  • Block users: Block from a user's profile; manage blocks in "Settings → Block List".
  • Contact: Use the email at the bottom of this page.

9. Security

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Access control via Firebase Security Rules (Firestore, Storage)
  • Minimized administrator privileges and access controls
  • Server-side processing via Cloud Functions Admin SDK

10. Children Under 14

The Service does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we discover that we have collected data from a child without parental consent, we will delete it immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as laws or the Service change. Material changes will be announced in-app or on this page in advance.
