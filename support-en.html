Frequently Asked Questions

How do I invite my family?

Go to My Page → Settings → Family Settings to find your invite code. Share it with your family; once they enter the code in the app, they join your household. If the code is exposed, tap the refresh icon to generate a new one. The previous code is invalidated immediately.

The AI included an ingredient I am allergic to.

First, check that the allergy is registered for that family member under Family Settings. If it is registered and the ingredient still appears, please email us and we will look into it.

Meal plans and recipes are generated automatically by AI. Always double-check ingredients and allergy information before cooking.

Is my family's information sent to an AI?

Yes. When generating a meal plan, each family member's name, gender, age, nationality, notes, allergies, and disliked foods, along with any request you type, are sent to Google Gemini. This happens only after you explicitly consent in the app; without consent, AI features are unavailable.

See section 6 of our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I withdraw my AI data consent?

Go to Settings → AI data sharing. You can withdraw at any time. The next time you use an AI feature, we will ask for consent again.

Why do I see ads?

All features of Jipgourmand are free. One interstitial ad is shown after every 5 AI meal plan generations. Ads never block you from using the app.

I found an inappropriate post or user.

Tap the menu at the top right of any community post and choose Report. To stop seeing a specific user, block them from their profile; manage blocks under Settings → Block List. Posts with enough reports are hidden automatically.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete Account. No support request is needed. Your profile, posts, comments, photos, meal plans, and household membership are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. See Account Deletion for details.

How do I turn off notifications?

Go to Settings → Notification Settings to toggle each notification type.
