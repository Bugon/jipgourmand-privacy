Account Deletion Request

App: Jipgourmand (com.jipgourmand.app)

Jipgourmand offers two ways for users to delete their account and associated data.

Option 1. Delete from within the app (recommended)

1. Launch Jipgourmand and sign in
2. Bottom tab My Page → top-right Settings icon
3. Tap Delete Account at the bottom
4. Confirm by tapping Delete in the dialog

This is processed immediately and all related data is permanently deleted, no separate contact required.

Option 2. Request via email

If you've already uninstalled the app or can no longer sign in, email us:

Email: bugon4713@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Include in the body:

  • The email or nickname you used to sign up
  • How you signed up (Google or Apple)

We process email requests within 7 business days and notify you by reply when complete.

Data that will be deleted

  • Account info (Firebase Auth credentials)
  • Profile (nickname, photo, bio)
  • All posts, comments, likes, and favorites you created
  • Meal plans, shopping lists, family member info
  • Household membership (if you are the last member, the entire household and its data are deleted)
  • Follow relationships, push notification tokens, block list

Data that may be retained

  • Anonymized statistical data (cannot identify individuals)
  • Information legally required to be retained for a specific period (e.g., e-commerce regulations)
